# Wallpaper Pro starter collection

15 original procedural wallpapers created for Wallpaper Pro and distributed under the repository's MIT license (Copyright 2026 Leif).

- 6 single-monitor images: 1920 × 1080.
- 6 dual-monitor panoramic images: 3840 × 1080, for a pair of side-by-side 16:9 displays.
- 3 silent H.264 looping videos: Northern Lights and Midnight Peaks panoramas at 3840 × 1080, plus Ember Dunes at 1920 × 1080. Each has an eight-second repeating camera movement.

Themes: Northern Lights, Ember Dunes, Midnight Peaks, Ocean Glass, Solar Bloom, and Indigo Horizon. These are stylized landscape illustrations; theme names describe their palettes.

No third-party photos or videos are included. `scripts/build-starter-library.py` deterministically creates the SVG scenes and renders the JPG, thumbnail, and MP4 assets using ffmpeg. No generation tools are needed to browse or use the bundled collection.
